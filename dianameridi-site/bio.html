About

Diana Meridi is a visual artist working at the intersection of technology, consciousness, and the metaphysical. Her work explores the liminal spaces between the digital and the transcendent, using mixed media and generative techniques to create immersive visual experiences.

Through her art, Diana investigates questions of identity, perception, and the nature of reality in an increasingly mediated world. Her pieces invite viewers to slow down and engage with layers of meaning that reveal themselves gradually.

Diana's work has been exhibited internationally and continues to evolve as she explores new tools and techniques for expressing the ineffable.
